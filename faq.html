02

Taste, Purity & Premium Justification

Healthy snacks usually taste like cardboard. What do MantraAQ snacks actually taste like?

We do not sacrifice taste for health. Traditional healthy snacks are air-puffed and dry; ours are not. MantraAQ uses a proprietary light seasoning process that bonds natural spices directly to our cold-pressed singhara chips.

The result: the same satisfying crunch and bold Indian flavour as traditional chips, with zero guilt, zero palm oil, and 100% clean ingredients.

I can buy regular singhara flour cheaper at the local market. Why pay a premium for MantraAQ?

Open-market singhara flour is prone to adulteration, grit, dirt, and mould from improper sun-drying by unorganised middlemen. MantraAQ runs an automated, certified facility: our water chestnuts are thoroughly washed, machine-dehulled, and cold-milled into an ultra-fine, grit-free flour.

You are paying for pure quality, zero digestive discomfort, and a reliable shelf life, not for the name.

03

Functional Health & Dietary Integrity

MantraAQ products are a natural, low-GI, gluten-free food and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. If you have diabetes, are pregnant, or are managing a medical condition, please consult your doctor or dietitian before making changes to your diet.

Are MantraAQ products truly diabetic-friendly, or is that just marketing?

It is backed by food science. Refined wheat flour (maida) has a high Glycemic Index of around 70+. Raw water chestnut inherently has a low-to-moderate GI, and our cold-processing prevents starch gelatinisation.

That means a portion of our snacks releases carbohydrates gradually into your bloodstream, avoiding the sharp insulin spikes typical of mainstream packaged snacks.

Are your products entirely gluten-free?

Yes. Water chestnut is a seed fruit, making it inherently 100% gluten-free and grain-free. Our entire facility is dedicated strictly to singhara-based production, eliminating cross-contamination risk with wheat or gluten.

Are your snacks safe for toddlers and pregnant individuals?

Yes, entirely. Our labels are fully transparent: no hidden maltodextrin, no INS chemical stabilisers, and no artificial sweeteners. Water chestnuts are rich in potassium, vitamin B6, and natural minerals, making them a nutrient-dense snack option for developing children and expecting mothers.

Does MantraAQ use MSG, artificial colours, or chemical preservatives?

Absolutely not. Our clean-label promise is strict: we rely entirely on natural cold-pressed spices, herbs, and rock salt for flavour. Zero MSG, zero trans-fats, zero palm oil, zero synthetic colours. What is on the ingredient list is exactly what you get.

04

Culinary Performance, Storage & Fasting (Vrat)

Can MantraAQ products be consumed during religious fasting (Vrat)?

Yes. Our pure water chestnut flours and snacks are ideal for fasting days like Navratri, Shivratri, and Ekadashi. Our strict clean-label, additive-free process keeps them fully compliant with Vrat dietary guidelines.

Singhara is naturally gluten-free: does your Singhara Pasta turn sticky or mushy when boiled?

Not at all. Standard gluten-free pastas often disintegrate when boiled because they lack binding agents. MantraAQ uses a proprietary extrusion process that keeps an al dente structural integrity: it holds its shape and absorbs sauces just like traditional durum wheat pasta.

Tip: boil for 6 to 8 minutes with a pinch of salt for the best texture.
What is the shelf life of the snacks and flours after opening?

Unopened, our nitrogen-flushed packages stay fresh for 9 months from manufacture. Once opened, transfer into a clean, airtight container and store somewhere cool and dry, and consume within 30 days for the best crunch and flavour.

Sourcing, Sustainability & Trade

Where does MantraAQ source its raw ingredients?

We source directly from local wetland farmers across Bihar, which accounts for over 40% of India's total singhara production. Bypassing traditional middlemen means fair pricing for farmers and immediate post-harvest processing to lock in freshness.

How can I trace where my food came from?

Every MantraAQ package carries a unique QR code. Scan it for full supply chain visibility, right down to the regional cluster of farms in Bihar where your crop was grown and harvested.

I am a distributor or modern trade buyer: what is your commercial shelf life and supply reliability?

All MantraAQ packaged snack SKUs carry a commercially viable 9-month shelf life, achieved naturally through nitrogen-flushing and high-barrier sustainable packaging, without a single artificial preservative.

Backed by direct-from-farm sourcing clusters across Bihar, we maintain year-round raw material stability, insulating retail partners from seasonal disruption or price volatility.
