Is MantraAQ Singhara Atta gluten free?

Yes. The only ingredient is water chestnut (singhara), which is naturally gluten free. Nothing else is added.

Can I eat singhara atta during a vrat or Navratri fast?

Yes. Singhara atta is a traditional fasting flour, and ours is 100% water chestnut with no wheat, maida or preservatives.

How do I make soft singhara rotis?

Knead the atta with warm water, rest it for a few minutes and roll it between two sheets of butter paper. Singhara dough has no gluten, so it needs gentle handling.
